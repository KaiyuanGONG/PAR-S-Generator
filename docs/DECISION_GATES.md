# PAR-S synthetic-data scientific decision gates

The software pipeline is complete without these experiments, but a production dataset must not be labelled physics-validated until the applicable gates pass. The packages under `experiments/validation-v1/` contain inputs, copied SMC variants, exact commands, run scripts, result templates and analyzers. They have **not** run SIMIND.

| Gate | Prepared package | Decision evidence | Pass criterion | Action if it fails |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Attenuation semantic and unit | `attenuation_ict` | Flag-15 `.ict` readback for uniform μ=0.05/0.15/0.30 plus attenuation-on/off analytical slab or water-cylinder ratios | Readback has a deterministic, documented mapping to the intended 140.5-keV linear attenuation coefficients; analytical and SIMIND ratios agree within a preregistered tolerance | Correct the `/FD` representation or SMC material mapping; never apply the audit's fitted ×1.8 as an automatic fix |
| Array axes and projection orientation | `asymmetric_fiducial` | Unambiguous asymmetric source positions in source volume, raw output and canonical viewer | Expected view/row/column order, rotation direction and flips are recovered without an unexplained reflection; half-voxel/phase convention is documented | Change the one canonical transform and migrate manifests/viewer together |
| Detector matrix and usable FOV | `fov_matrix` | Clean runs at 128, 160 and 208 with actual errors, headers, support and truncation recorded | Choose the smallest stable matrix that covers the protocol-required object and is consistent with the GE 393.6×511.7-mm specification; quantify any retained crop | Repair SMC/index coupling or document a measured crop; do not infer success from directory names |
| Collimator–detector response and sensitivity | `point_line_source` | Point and line source sensitivity, spatial FWHM and acquisition geometry | Agreement with correctly matched GE 870 CZT WEHR conditions within a preregistered tolerance; report geometry and energy-window differences | Revisit collimator/detector SMC parameters before production |
| Monte Carlo sampling distribution | `rr_nn_ladder` | At least five distinct `/RR` realizations at `/NN` 1, 5 and 10; voxelwise repeated-sampling mean/variance and convergence | Independent streams are confirmed; expectation stability and variance scaling with `/NN` are characterized | Change history count or seed policy; do not estimate Fano factors from spatial variation in one image |
| Activity–time contract | protocol review plus one calibrated pilot | Resolve the folder label “60 MBq × 20 s” against SMC Index-25=1704 (60 MBq × 28.4 s) and record projections/view timing | One internally consistent activity, duration, number-of-views and SIMIND scaling definition approved for the current protocol | Update labels and effective config; regenerate affected projections |
| Clinical count scale and observation status | count-calibration pilot | Measured or otherwise defensible projection totals under the chosen protocol | Scale provenance and uncertainty are recorded; only then set observation status to `verified` | Keep expectation and any Poisson realization labelled `research` or `toy` |

Additional dataset acceptance gates remain automatic: all phantoms pass containment/non-overlap/size/TNR QC; all projection files pass exact size, dtype, header, result-marker and command-token checks; split identifiers are fixed before observations; and the final manifest checksum is valid.

Claims unlocked by these gates are narrow: current synthetic liver SPECT and the explicitly tested GE NM/CT 870 CZT protocol. They do not establish validity for other organs, cancers, scanners, collimators or acquisition protocols.
